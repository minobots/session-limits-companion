Privacy Policy — Session Limits

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Session Limits is a dashboard that shows how much of your AI coding subscriptions you have used and when they reset. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • There is no Session Limits server. The app talks only to the AI providers' own APIs and, optionally, to a companion program you run on your own Mac.
  • Your API keys and sign-in tokens never leave your device except in a request to the provider that issued them.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What the app stores on your device

DataWhere it is keptWhy
Provider API keys / OAuth tokens (MiniMax, OpenCode Go, Claude) The operating system's secure store — Keychain on iOS/macOS, the Keystore-backed encrypted store on Android To read your usage from that provider
Companion app address and access token Secure store (token) and app preferences (address) To reach a companion you have set up
Which services you have enabled, and manually entered values App preferencesTo render your dashboard
A rolling ~7-day local history of usage percentages App preferencesTo draw the per-provider trend and heatmap

All of this stays on the device. Uninstalling the app removes it. None of it is transmitted to the app's developer.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • To each AI provider you connect, an HTTPS request to read your current usage — governed by that provider's own privacy policy. The app contacts only:
    • api.minimax.io / api.minimaxi.com — MiniMax
    • opencode.ai — OpenCode Go
    • claude.com / platform.claude.com / api.anthropic.com — Claude (standard OAuth sign-in, then usage reads with the resulting token)
  • To your companion app, if you set one up, an authenticated request to the address you entered (typically a machine on your own network or private Tailscale network). That program runs on your hardware; it is not operated by the app's developer.
  • Nothing else. The app makes no other network connections.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no backend, no telemetry, and no crash reporting that sends data off the device. The developer cannot see your keys, your usage, or that you use the app.

Platform-level data

The app stores are operated by Apple and Google. When you download or update the app, or if the operating system reports a crash, those companies may process data under their own policies. The app itself does not add to this.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published at the same location.
